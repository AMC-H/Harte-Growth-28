How we build AI follow-up on WhatsApp for small businesses

Not a chatbot asking "how can I help you?". An AI that summarises enquiries, pings you, and confirms to the customer within 30 seconds.

The problem we solve

A good enquiry comes in while you're in a meeting. By the time you call back, the customer has already gone elsewhere.

How it works, step by step

  1. Someone fills in a short intake form on your site.
  2. WhatsApp opens automatically with a pre-filled message.
  3. AI summarises the enquiry and adds it to your CRM.
  4. You get a notification with that summary.
  5. The customer gets an automatic confirmation within 30 seconds.

Why AI, and not just an auto-reply?

An auto-reply sends everyone the same message. AI personalises the confirmation, qualifies the enquiry, summarises it in a way you can use, and flags when something is urgent. A fixed rule can't do that.

What we deliberately don't do

We don't build chatbots that "have conversations" with the customer. An AI pretending to think along feels fake faster than a simple, honest confirmation that the enquiry has been received.

The technology behind it

WhatsApp Business API, Make or n8n for the connections between systems, OpenAI or Anthropic for the summary, and a connection to your CRM, usually Pipedrive or HubSpot.

What it costs

Setting it up takes 1 to 3 weeks. Ongoing, expect around €30 to €80 a month.

AI follow-up only makes sense if the process behind it is right. Fix the process first, then automate.
