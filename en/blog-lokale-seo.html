Local SEO for service businesses: where we start

Starting local SEO from scratch? This order gets you the first visible results within 6 to 12 weeks.

Local SEO comes down to three things Google needs to know: that you exist, where you are, and whether you're trustworthy enough to show to local searchers. We go through these five steps in this order, not at random, but because each step builds on the one before.

Step 1. Get your Google Business Profile in order

This profile is usually the first thing a potential customer sees, even before your website. Make sure your name, address and phone number are consistent everywhere your business appears online, and that you have the right categories, a correct service area, real photos (no stock photos), up-to-date opening hours and full descriptions for each service.

Step 2. A dedicated page for each area

Every area you serve deserves its own page: "service in town" as the title, 200 to 400 words of unique local copy (not copied from one town to the next), a map, and examples of projects you did there.

Step 3. Reviews, with a target

Aim for 10 or more reviews with an average of 4.5 stars within three months. Ask for them in person, right after a successful project, not in one generic email afterwards that nobody opens.

Step 4. The technical foundation

Schema.org LocalBusiness markup, an up-to-date sitemap, speed on mobile and a valid HTTPS certificate. Invisible to visitors, essential to how Google judges your page.

Step 5. Visible in ChatGPT and Gemini too

Mentions on authoritative sites in your industry don't just help in Google, but increasingly in the answers AI search engines give too. That's called GEO, generative engine optimization, and it builds on exactly the same trust signals as classic local SEO.

Weeks 1–4: technical foundation and Google Business Profile. Weeks 4–8: first local pages live and indexed. Weeks 8–12: first visible movement in rankings. Expected timeline

After that, the effect keeps building: the longer the pages are online and the more reviews and mentions you gain, the stronger your position becomes.
