Cookie statement

What we use and why. Analytics cookies load only if you actively agree. Decline and no external script is loaded.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. Sites use cookies to remember, for instance, your language preference or to anonymously measure which pages get most traffic.
