What the scan checks.

  1. 1

    SEO foundations

    Title, meta description, canonical, headings, language code, structured data. Everything Google needs to show you properly.

    Speed

    Load time, page weight, render-blocking scripts. Every second of delay costs you 7% in conversions. Google knows that too.

    Mobile

    60% of your visitors are on mobile. We check the viewport, text size, tap targets and whether everything fits properly.

    Accessibility

    Alt text, contrast, focus states, ARIA. It matters for everyone who doesn't use a mouse, and for Google.
