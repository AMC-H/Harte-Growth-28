→What the scan checks
01

SEO foundation

Title, meta description, canonical, headings, language code, structured data. Everything Google needs to show you well.

02

Speed

Load time, size, blocking scripts. Every second of delay costs you 7% conversion. Google knows that too.

03

Mobile

60% of your visitors are on mobile. We check viewport, text size, tap targets and whether everything fits properly.

Accessibility

Alt text, contrast, focus states, ARIA. Important for anyone who doesn't use a mouse, and for Google.
