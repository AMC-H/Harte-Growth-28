Frequently asked

How much material do I need?

Around 5 to 6 photos or a few short clips is enough for one video. Got more? Send it all, and we'll pick what works best.

How do I send my files?

Via WeTransfer or Google Drive. Send us the link on WhatsApp or by email. Not through WhatsApp itself: it compresses the files and the quality drops.

Can I use the video anywhere?

Yes, the video is yours. Use it on social media, your website, in a listing or in ads.

What if I'm not happy?

One round of revisions is included. If we can't sort it out together, you get your money back.
