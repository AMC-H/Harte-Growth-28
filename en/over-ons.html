About us

Growth is a process, not a project.

A team that builds growth marketing for businesses that want to keep growing. We are selective: we only take you on if your organisation fits our approach.

Get your free growth scan How we work

One team, one system, one point of contact.

Harte Growth is a growth marketing agency for businesses that are serious about growing, from ambitious owners to organisations with their own marketing team. We work from the Netherlands and the Costa Blanca in Spain, and remotely across Europe. You can talk to us in Dutch, English or Spanish.

We don't do one-off jobs. We build one system: video and content draw attention, the website turns visitors into enquiries, local SEO and AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity bring traffic, and every enquiry gets followed up straight away, including on WhatsApp.

You get one fixed point of contact who knows your business. No account manager passing you along, no changing faces, and reporting in plain language.

Our philosophy.

I

Revenue over attention.

Reach is nice, enquiries are what count. We focus on what actually moves your revenue.

II

One system, not separate islands.

Website, SEO, content and automation only work together if they are built that way.

III

Measure, then decide.

Every month, in black and white, what worked and what didn't. No jargon, no 40 charts.

IV

Honest about results.

We don't promise the top spot in Google. What you do get, every month, is a clear account of what we did and what it delivered.

V

Multilingual from day one.

Dutch, English and Spanish are our working languages. We build websites in up to five languages, for businesses with customers from several countries.

VI

No long contracts.

Monthly plans can be cancelled monthly, with one month's notice. Videos and automations are paid once, at a fixed price agreed upfront.

Questions about working together.

How quickly will I see results?

You get a video within 48 hours and a website goes live within two weeks. With local SEO you usually see the first results within 6 to 12 weeks.

Am I tied into anything?

No. Monthly plans can be cancelled monthly with one month's notice. Videos and automations are a one-off payment.

Do you work in Spain and in several languages?

Yes. We work from the Netherlands and the Costa Blanca, and remotely across Europe. You can talk to us in Dutch, English or Spanish, and we build websites in up to five languages.

Do you promise a top spot in Google?

No, nobody can honestly promise that. What you do get is a monthly one-page overview of what we did and what it delivered, in plain language.

Why not just hire a freelancer or a big agency?

A freelancer often does one thing well; a big agency splits the work across departments. We build website, SEO, content and follow-up as one system, with one fixed point of contact. The comparison above shows the difference.
