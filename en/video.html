Common questions.

Is this everything you make?

No, this is a small sample. Every video is made for that one business, so yours gets its own style, music and message. Tell us on WhatsApp what you have in mind and we'll tell you straight away if we can make it.

How much material do I need?

Around 5 or 6 photos or a few short clips is enough for one video. Got more? Send it all, we'll pick what works best.

How do I send my files?

Ideally via WeTransfer. Put your photos and clips in and send us the link on WhatsApp or by email. Google Drive works too. Please don't send them one by one on WhatsApp: it shrinks the images and the quality drops.

Can I use the video anywhere?

Yes, the video is yours. Use it on social media, your website, in an ad or in a listing.

What if I'm not happy?

One round of revisions is included. If we still can't get it right together, you get your money back.

Do you also make several videos a month?

Yes. If you want regular content every month, we agree a fixed monthly price. Send us a message and we'll work it out for you.
